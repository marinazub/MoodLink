# Independent human validation

Open Human_Validation_Blinded.xlsx. Do not open the coordinator key, repository coding files, or agreement report until your initial ratings are saved.

1. Read the Codebook sheet. These definitions are frozen for this validation pass; record suggested changes in notes rather than silently changing the rule.
2. Select a human case (H001–H070). Read ALL its assignment rows in Assignments. Model X and Model Y conceal model identity. Some cases contain only one model.
3. Use only rationale, critic_rationale, and critic_coverage_rationale. Assigned constructs are indexing metadata, not independent mechanism evidence. No original transcript is supplied or to be inferred as additional evidence.
4. For each of the21 codes, enter yes or unclear in Ratings. Yes requires an explicit supporting passage. Unclear means the available text does not establish the mechanism; it is not confirmed absence. Leave a rating blank only when you have not completed it.
5. For yes, enter assignment ID(s), field name(s), an exact quote of no more than40 words per excerpt, and your reviewer identifier. If a code compares the two models, cite both sides; add additional short excerpts in the memo as needed. For unclear, enter your reviewer identifier; a memo is optional.
6. A01 and A02 require a comparison between the two models. A coder rationale and its own critic do not constitute a paired-model case. Critic disagreement does not prove a construct is wrong. Assignment-level coverage does not establish coverage of the whole model set.
7. Filter Ratings by human case or code to organize your work. Check that Completion shows Complete for every row. Save your completed workbook under a new name. Keep the blank packet unchanged.
8. Only after saving initial judgments should you compare with agent decisions. Any discussion or adjudication belongs in a separate file; do not overwrite your initial ratings.

This packet contains70 meaning units and248 assignment rows. The coordinator can reconnect them to the original workbook through a separately held key. Your task is to evaluate written explanations, not to judge either model's overall clinical or coding accuracy.
