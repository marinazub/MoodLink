# Revised interpretation: stated evidence thresholds and assignment actions

The previous analysis underused the information in No Match. Under the stated prompt, it is the intended abstention action when the current construct packet has insufficient evidence. A model assigning where the other abstains therefore supplies a **design-based sufficiency contrast**, even if the abstaining model gives no rationale. The available rationale and critic texts help identify which interpretive moves accompany that contrast; they do not reveal the absent model’s exact rejected inference.

## What can and cannot be concluded

The model’s observable “actions” are assignments and exported abstentions; its “mind” can only be approached through its stated rationales. Critics add a separate evaluative perspective. Those outputs may expose inferential distance, shifting standards or unresolved contradiction, but are not a direct record of hidden cognition. The critique may be produced by a different model or stage; do not call every proposal–critic difference internal self-contradiction.

The current source code also shows why the export needs qualification: no coding results can follow parse failure; core and outcome assignments are exported separately; interviewer questions are excluded in the current exporter. These are possible provenance distinctions, not asserted explanations for individual historical No Match rows. Equal packet exposure and the historical runtime configuration have not been established here.

The prior inter-agent agreement figures and human-validation packet belong to version1. They remain unchanged for auditability and must not be presented as validation of this revised codebook. Version2 introduces new action/review codes and changes the A01 application; independent and human validation should be rerun on version2 before claiming its reliability.
